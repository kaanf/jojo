## Implementation

**Close gate (`Tx::admission`).** An open or confirmed close ticket of the current epoch is a layer over the stored RUN/HOLD admission intent. Effective admission is closed while the layer exists. The scheduler scan and `issue_process_launch` already check effective admission. Two schema-v10 triggers refuse a new `scheduler_attempts` row and a `scheduler-launch` ISSUED_UNKNOWN transition while the gate holds. Cancelling a close removes only the layer: the stored intent, queue intents and user pauses/cancels are untouched.

**Commands (`jojo-storage::shutdown`).** Each command is one writer transaction:

- `prepare_close` (human origin). Snapshots active work: current-epoch scheduler attempts that are not sealed (reserved, starting, running, stopping or settling). Queued-only work is not active.
  - No active work: resolves `NO_ACTIVE` without any confirmation.
  - Active work: returns `CONFIRM_REQUIRED` and waits for a human resolution.
  - Repeated quits return the existing operation (`EXISTING`).
- `cancel_close`. Records `CANCELLED`. It sends nothing. A cancelled ticket is expired; a confirmed one cannot be cancelled ("issued stops cannot be undone").
- `confirm_close`. Records `CONFIRMED` and a shutdown ID, then freezes the target set from the current state at commit time. A reserved or starting attempt therefore cannot vanish from tracking. Each target whose tree is not already proven absent gets the existing P1-10 shutdown stop intent:
  - It is classified INTERRUPTED, never a permanent task cancel.
  - A reserved, never-issued attempt is retired as NOT_STARTED; its queue item stays.
  - Already-exited work is not stopped and keeps its real result.
- `emergency_close` (GUI stdio EOF, no human). Closes the stored admission intent. It supersedes an open user ticket with a confirmed `DISCONNECT` ticket and freezes targets the same way.
- Tickets bind the epoch. A ticket from an earlier epoch is refused in Rust and by SQL trigger.

**Parallel stop.** The coordinator never signals. Each P1-09 runner polls the durable stop intent and escalates on its own retained lifetime handle (capability INT, then TERM, then verified KILL, with the injected U05 budgets). Runners stop in parallel. `CloseWait::for_budgets` bounds the wait to one runner's full escalation plus polling, independent of the number of targets.

**Finalization (`finish_close` → `Tx::finalize_shutdown`).**

1. Waits within the budget, on the injected clock, until no target is still stopping.
2. Settles targets proven absent through P1-10.
3. Commits one receipt, computed inside the writer transaction by the pure `jojo-domain::shutdown::verdict`.

Rules:

- **Stopped** means only one of: TREE_ABSENT, NOT_SPAWNED, a retired never-issued reservation, or a sealed recovered result. STOP_UNCONFIRMED, SPAWN_FAILED or no conclusion is a failure.
- **Persisted** means a sealed result and no EVIDENCE_LOSS. Other failures:
  - live claims
  - unsealed attempts outside the target set
  - earlier-epoch launches that are neither settled nor proven absent
- **READY** commits in the same transaction as the CLEAN session marker. It needs every check to pass and a user origin.
- **EMERGENCY** is a disconnect that confirmed and sealed everything. The session stays unclean, so the next start runs recovery inspection.
- **FAILED** keeps claims, the admission hold and the unclean marker. `finish_close` may be retried, and only unresolved targets can change.
- Each receipt records `process_stop_confirmed`, `persistence_sealed`, per-target stop facts (a forced KILL appears as `Stopped { forced: true }`), recovery items (interrupted results, ISSUED_UNKNOWN effects, holds) and failures.
- An `Err` from `finish_close` means no receipt was committed and nothing may be acknowledged.

**Schema version 10** adds `close_tickets`, `close_resolutions`, `shutdown_targets` and `shutdown_receipts`, each with update/delete triggers. Insert guards:

- A ticket needs the running epoch and no other open close.
- A resolution needs the ticket's epoch to be running. A `DISCONNECT` ticket cannot be cancelled.
- A non-FAILED receipt needs no live claim and every target sealed. READY also needs a user origin. At most one final receipt per shutdown.
- CHECKs tie FAILED to non-empty failures, and READY/EMERGENCY to both flags set.

T12 tests cover the following (brief T12 scenarios in brackets):

- **Idle close** [queued-only work retained]. With one sealed success and one retired reservation, close resolves without confirmation and commits READY with the CLEAN marker atomically. The writer is then fenced. The queue is identical after reopen, the previous session is CLEAN and admission is closed.
- **Dialog open** [active count changes; cancelled close continues jobs]. A settling attempt sealing SUCCEEDED changes the count from two to one without confirming. A repeated quit shares the ticket. Cancel restores effective admission and leaves queue state unchanged. The cancelled ticket is expired. The running job got no STOP_REQUESTED or signal, finished on its own and has no stop cause.
- **Confirmed close** [two provider trees plus test worker stop]. A Claude tree, a Codex tree and a Validator-role worker all reach RUNNING, then stop in parallel through INT. The close is READY, all three are INTERRUPTED(shutdown) and listed as recovery items, no queue intent is CANCEL, and no claim remains after reopen. A confirmed close cannot be cancelled.
- **Escalation** [timeout then verified KILL]. A signal-ignoring tree is KILLed after the graceful budgets. The receipt shows `Stopped { forced: true }` and the observation reads "graceful stop failed; forced stop confirmed". The result is INTERRUPTED, never success.
- **Unconfirmed stop** [denied/unknown child stop leaves failure and claims]. An escaped stdout holder yields STOP_UNCONFIRMED. The close commits FAILED (process stop not confirmed) and claims and the gate are kept. Cancel is refused and the session stays RUNNING/unclean. A retry commits a second FAILED receipt and seals nothing.
- **Disk failure** [unknown stop/disk failure cannot acknowledge]. An injected log spool failure gives FAILED with `persistence_sealed=false` even though the process stopped.
- **Pipe EOF** [stops admission without user confirmation]. With a user dialog open, the emergency stop supersedes it with no human input and interrupts the running attempt. A reserved attempt is retired with no process. The receipt is EMERGENCY, the session is not CLEAN, and the next start reports the previous session UNCLEAN. Queued work is retained.
- **Reservation/spawn races**. An open gate refuses the issue boundary, and no launch row is written. With one attempt issued-not-spawned and one spawned-not-registered when Confirm commits, the target set has all three. The first becomes NOT_SPAWNED, the second stops at registration and the reserved one is NOT_STARTED. The close is READY.
- **Success/stop race**. A success sealed before close is unchanged. An attempt that exited before Confirm gets no stop intent and seals SUCCEEDED with its candidate during finalization.
- **Expired/old epoch**. An earlier-epoch ticket cannot be confirmed or cancelled and does not gate the new epoch. The earlier epoch's QUARANTINED claims make the new close FAILED.
- **SQL guards**. Raw SQL refuses a second open ticket, a ticket for a non-running epoch, the issue boundary and new reservations under the gate, cancelling a disconnect, clean receipts with a live claim, inconsistent flags, and every update/delete.

## Limits and next gate

- The coordinator is a storage library driven by its caller. Not part of P1-12: the helper worker pool, stdio EOF detection, ShutdownReady transport, and the helper-exit handshake (P1-13), and the AppKit quit flow (P1-14).
- Retry is re-evaluation. A target whose runner reported STOP_UNCONFIRMED, or whose runner died, has no lifetime handle left. It stays failed until reopen inspection (P1-11) proves absence. Approved stronger escalation of an unhandled orphan is not added.
- Any live claim blocks READY, by the unchanged P1-02 clean-shutdown rule. This includes QUARANTINED claims of earlier-epoch work kept on Hold, so such a close commits FAILED even when nothing local is running. This is fail-closed, but a user with held recovery items cannot quit cleanly until they resolve them.
- A disconnect during an already user-confirmed close is recorded by the EMERGENCY receipt (`finish_close(disconnected=true)`) rather than by a separate ticket.
- Shutdown-retired reservations keep their queue item with intent HOLD (the existing retirement rule). Restart needs a human resume and never dispatches automatically.
- Process groups are not hostile-code containment. Escaped unrecorded descendants are detected only when they hold an output stream open.
- Immutability is policy plus SQL triggers and digests, not protection against a hostile same-user process.
